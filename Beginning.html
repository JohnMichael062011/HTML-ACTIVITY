As Minokawa reached the heavens, it opened its mighty beak and swallowed the moon whole. Night fell too early, and fear spread among the people. Crops wilted, animals hid, and the stars seemed to tremble. Elders remembered ancient warnings and realized that Minokawa had returned to claim the light. The people gathered together, refusing to surrender to the darkness. They beat drums, clanged pots, rang bells, and cried out prayers that echoed into the sky. Their voices carried hope, courage, and defiance, rising like a storm against the wings of the great bird. Startled by the thunder of human unity, Minokawa released the moon, which returned to its place, wounded but shining. Yet the creature was not satisfied. At another time, it returned and reached for the sun itself, casting the world into an even deeper darkness, as if the day had been erased..
