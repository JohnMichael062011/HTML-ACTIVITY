RECOMMENDATION

To promote sustainable packaging solutions, it is recommended that manufacturers adopt a "waste-to-wealth" strategy by utilizing a starch-blended biopolymer consisting of banana peel and corn starch. This research indicates that while banana peels provide a viable organic starch source (approx. 18.5%), the inherent brittleness and high water affinity of native starch are significantly mitigated by the addition of corn starch as a co-biopolymer. To ensure optimal structural integrity, processors must implement strict quality controls to source banana peels before they reach the over-ripe stage, preventing the conversion of starch into glucose. Furthermore, the use of Differential Scanning Calorimetry (DSC) and FTIR spectroscopy is advised to standardize the thermal transitions and chemical bonding of the films, ensuring they can withstand the mechanical rigors of the packaging industry. Implementing this blend not only reduces the environmental persistence of non-biodegradable plastics like PVC but also provides a high-value outlet for organic waste, thereby reducing landfill volumes and supporting Malaysia’s sustainable manufacturing goals.
