RECOMMENDATION

Fagraea fragrans Roxb. essential oil be utilized as a dual-functional ingredient in luxury perfumery and protective skincare. To achieve a maximum commercial yield of 0.35% v/w, extraction should be standardized to a 7.5 mL g⁻¹ water-to-flower ratio over a 215-minute hydro-distillation period. Given its classification as a top-to-middle note with a very strong aroma, the oil is ideal for providing the primary "heart" of floral fragrances. Furthermore, its moderate antioxidant activity and proven non-toxicity to human cells (up to 250 μg mL⁻¹) make it a safe and effective bioactive additive for "clean beauty" formulations aimed at oxidative stress protection.
