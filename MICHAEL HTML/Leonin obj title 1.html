Objective

The aim of this study was to investigate the skin anti‐inflammatory activity of rose petal extract (RPE) and the mechanisms underlying this phenomenon. Recently, flowers have been considered as dietary resources owing to their biological activities, such as inhibition of nephritis and hemorrhoids. Herein, we confirmed the skin anti‐inflammatory activity of RPE upon solar UV (sUV) exposure.RPE reduced sUV‐induced COX‐2 expression as well as expressions of several cytokines.
