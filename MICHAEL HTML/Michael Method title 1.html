METHODOLOGY

The researchers conducted a comprehensive monitoring program by collecting 1-liter spot water samples from nineteen sites along the two river catchments on two separate occasions in 2019 (March and June). To isolate the organic pollutants, the samples were processed using solid-phase extraction with HLB-L sorbent disks. The analysis utilized a high-resolution dual-instrumentation approach, combining liquid chromatography-quadrupole-time-of-flight mass spectrometry (LC-QToF-MS) and gas chromatography-mass spectrometry (GC-MS). Detected compounds were then identified by screening the results against extensive commercially available chemical databases. This "suspect screening" method allowed the team to detect a broad range of substances with varying physico-chemical properties without needing to pre-select a small group of target chemicals.
