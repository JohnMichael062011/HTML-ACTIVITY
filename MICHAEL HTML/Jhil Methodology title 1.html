METHODOLOGY

A mathematical model generated through CCD was used to predict the essential oil yield. Experimental validation was performed under optimal conditions (7.5 mL g⁻¹ water-to-flower ratio and 215 minutes distillation time), and predicted yields were compared with experimental results. The study employed an experimental research design using a factorial approach combined with Response Surface Methodology (RSM). A Central Composite Design (CCD) was utilized to optimize the hydro-distillation extraction parameters of essential oil from Fagraea fragrans Roxb. flowers.
