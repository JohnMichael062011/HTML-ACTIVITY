Statement of the Problem

  1. What are the optimal hydro-distillation conditions for maximizing the essential oil yield from Fagraea fragrans Roxb. flowers?
  2. How accurately can a Central Composite Design (CCD) predict the essential oil yield under optimized conditions?
  3. What are the major chemical constituents present in the extracted essential oil?
  4. Does the essential oil exhibit antioxidant activity suitable for cosmetic applications?
  5. Is the essential oil non-toxic to human cells at concentrations relevant to cosmetic and perfumery use?
