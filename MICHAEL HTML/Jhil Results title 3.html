RESULTS

The study highlights that banana peels are a rich source of starch, containing approximately 18.5%, though the ripening stage is critical because over-ripe peels convert their starch into glucose, which is less suitable for plastic formation. By introducing corn as a co-biopolymer, the research addresses the inherent limitations of native starch films, such as their tendency to be too brittle or absorb too much moisture. The expected outcome is a bioplastic that maintains structural integrity while remaining fully biodegradable. The integration of two different starch sources represents a "waste-to-wealth" strategy, suggesting that organic waste from the food industry can be successfully transformed into alternative resources for the plastic manufacturing sector, thereby reducing landfill volume and environmental pollution.
