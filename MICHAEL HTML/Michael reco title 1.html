RECOMMENDATION

To protect the ecological integrity of the River Itchen and River Test catchments, it is recommended that local environmental agencies shift from traditional targeted monitoring to a "suspect screening" approach using LC-QToF-MS and GC-MS instrumentation. This methodology allows for the detection of a broader chemical profile—as evidenced by the 231 pollutants identified in this study—including emerging contaminants on the EU Water Framework Directive Watch List. Resource management should be strategically tiered: Wastewater Treatment Plants (WWTPs) must be prioritized for pharmaceutical and industrial chemical mitigation, while agricultural policies should address seasonal runoff, specifically targeting herbicides in spring and insecticides in summer. Given that these rivers are sensitive chalk streams, integrated toxicological assessments are urgently needed to determine the cumulative "cocktail effect" of these mixtures on macroinvertebrate biodiversity, ensuring that these Sites of Special Scientific Interest (SSSI) remain resilient against chronic anthropogenic pressure.
