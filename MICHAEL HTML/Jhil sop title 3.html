Statement of the Problem

  1. How can the environmental persistence and the increasing production of non-biodegradable synthetic plastics, such as polyethylene and polyvinyl chloride, be reduced through the use of natural biopolymers?
  2. In what ways can the massive amounts of organic waste generated by the banana processing industry in Malaysia be diverted from landfills to prevent environmental harm and respiratory health issues?
  3. To what extent can the high water affinity and inherent brittleness of native starch-based films be improved by combining starches from two different sources, specifically banana peel and corn?
  4. What is the impact of the ripening stage of banana peels on starch concentration, and how does the conversion of starch to glucose in over-ripe peels affect the suitability of the material for bioplastic production?
  5. How do the tensile properties and thermal stability of a banana peel/corn starch blend compare to traditional plastics, and can this combination meet the functional requirements of the packaging industry?
