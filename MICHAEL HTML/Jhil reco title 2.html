RECOMMENDATION

To optimize the efficiency of automated waste management systems, it is recommended to implement a hybrid robotic framework that integrates AlexNet-based Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) with specialized mechanical end-effectors tailored to material types. For high-accuracy classification, the system should utilize a multi-layered CNN architecture—comprising convolutional, pooling, and fully connected layers—to process visual data and categorize waste into distinct streams such as glass, plastic, and carton. Practically, the choice of manipulation hardware should be driven by material characteristics: suction mechanisms are highly effective for non-porous materials like plastic (achieving a 91% success rate) but may fail on high-profile items due to stress; conversely, mechanical grabbers are superior for glass (87.78% success rate) but face spatial constraints with smaller fragments. To further enhance the economic viability of the operation, the integration of weight sensors at the input stage is advised to provide real-time mass measurements and immediate byproduct valuation, ensuring a transparent and data-driven reusable waste framework.
