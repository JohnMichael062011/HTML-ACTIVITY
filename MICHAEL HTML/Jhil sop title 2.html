Statement of the Problem

  1. How can an AI-based robotic system accurately identify and classify reusable waste materials using visual data?
  2. How can appropriate grabbing or suction mechanisms be selected based on the spatial and material characteristics of waste items?
  3. What challenges affect the efficiency of robotic gripping and suction when handling glass, plastic, and carton materials?
  4. How effective is a CNN-based model, such as AlexNet, in improving waste material recognition and segregation accuracy?
  5. How can automated measurement and classification enhance the reuse and economic valuation of waste materials?
