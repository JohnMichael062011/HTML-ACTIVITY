Statement of the Problem

  1. How does the presence of a complex mixture of over 231 organic pollutants, including those on the EU "Watch List," impact the water quality of the River Itchen and River Test?
  2. To what extent do specific anthropogenic activities, such as wastewater treatment and agricultural practices, contribute to the diverse chemical profiles found in these chalk streams?
  3. How do seasonal variations between spring and summer influence the dominance of different pollutant types, such as the shift from herbicides and fungicides to insecticides?
  4. What are the potential ecological risks to macroinvertebrate biodiversity and other sensitive species within these designated Sites of Special Scientific Interest (SSSI)?
  5. In what ways can a "suspect screening" approach effectively identify unregulated or emerging contaminants that traditional monitoring might overlook?
