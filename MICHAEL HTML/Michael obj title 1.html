OBJECTIVES

The primary objective of this study is to investigate the occurrence and distribution of organic pollutants within the River Itchen and River Test catchments in Southern England. The research seeks to identify and categorize a wide array of chemicals—specifically pharmaceuticals, personal care products (PPCPs), plant protection products (PPPs), and industrial chemicals—to establish a comprehensive chemical profile for these chalk streams. Additionally, the study aims to compare seasonal fluctuations in pollutant types between March and June to pinpoint the primary sources of contamination, whether from point-source wastewater discharges or diffuse agricultural runoff. Ultimately, the findings are intended to provide a critical baseline for future toxicological investigations to assess the long-term ecological health of these protected freshwater ecosystems.
