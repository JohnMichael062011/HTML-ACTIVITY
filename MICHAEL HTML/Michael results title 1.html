RESULTS

The study successfully identified a total of 231 organic pollutants, comprising 115 pharmaceuticals and personal care products, 81 plant protection products, and 35 industrial chemicals. The results revealed a distinct seasonal pattern in chemical dominance: herbicides and fungicides were most prevalent during the spring sampling, while insecticides were found more frequently in the summer. Both rivers exhibited remarkably similar chemical profiles, suggesting consistent environmental pressures across the region. Wastewater treatment plants were confirmed as the primary source of pharmaceuticals, whereas agricultural runoff was the main contributor of plant protection products. Notably, the mixture included several "priority substances" and chemicals currently on the EU Water Framework Directive's third Watch List, highlighting an urgent need for further research into their combined impact on macroinvertebrate biodiversity.
