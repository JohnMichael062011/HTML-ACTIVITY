OBJECTIVES

This research aims to investigate the potential of combining banana peel starch and corn starch to create a reinforced bioplastic film. Specifically, the study seeks to evaluate the tensile properties of these blended films to ensure they meet mechanical requirements for packaging. It also intends to determine the maximum water uptake of the material to assess its durability in moist environments. Furthermore, the research aims to characterize the films in terms of their functional groups and thermal properties using advanced spectroscopic and calorimetric techniques. Ultimately, the study intends to demonstrate how organic waste can be repurposed to reduce environmental impact and support the development of sustainable industries in Malaysia.
