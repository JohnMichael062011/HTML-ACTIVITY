Methodology

The study employed an experimental research design to examine the effect of sunlight exposure on the growth of mung bean plants. Fifteen mung bean seeds were planted in identical pots containing the same type and amount of soil to ensure uniform growing conditions. The plants were divided into three groups based on sunlight exposure: Group A received four hours of sunlight per day, Group B received eight hours per day, and Group C received twelve hours per day. All plants were watered equally on a daily basis to control for moisture variation. Plant height was measured weekly using a ruler over a period of four weeks. The collected data were recorded, and the average height of the plants in each group was calculated and compared using descriptive statistical methods to determine the effect of sunlight duration on plant growth.
