Statement of the Problem

  1. How has the simplification of agricultural landscapes through land expansion and the removal of non-crop habitats contributed to the rapid decline in farmland biodiversity?
  2. To what extent does the reduction of landscape complexity and non-crop habitat availability impair the functioning of natural pest control services?
  3. In what ways does the loss of field edges and herbaceous or wooded habitats diminish the exchange of natural enemies between crop and non-crop environments?
  4. Is there a consistent correlation between landscape composition and the actual reduction of crop injury, or is this relationship poorly documented in current research?
  5. Which specific landscape features—such as patchiness, wooded areas, or herbaceous margins—are most critical for sustaining natural enemy populations and stabilizing pest pressure?
