Results

Based on the findings of the study, it is recommended that mung bean plants be provided with moderate sunlight exposure, specifically around eight hours per day, to promote optimal growth. Gardeners and farmers should avoid prolonged sunlight exposure that may cause plant stress and inhibit healthy development. Future research should explore additional factors such as sunlight intensity, soil type, and watering frequency to gain a more comprehensive understanding of plant growth conditions. It is also recommended that similar studies be conducted over a longer period and with a larger sample size to improve the reliability and accuracy of the results. Plants receiving only 4 hours of sunlight showed slower growth, while plants exposed to 12 hours of sunlight showed signs of stress, such as yellowing leaves. This indicates that moderate sunlight exposure promotes optimal growth.
