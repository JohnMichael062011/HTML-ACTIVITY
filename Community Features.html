Community Features

TOP 5 COMMUNITY ISSUES


1. Flooding during heavy rains – Some areas in the barangay experience flooding because of 
   its proximity to the Marikina River, especially during typhoon season. 
   This affects transportation and the safety of residents.

2. Traffic congestion – Busy streets and increasing vehicles cause delays for commuters,
   especially during rush hours when people go to work or school.

3. Waste management – Improper disposal of garbage and lack of segregation can 
   lead to pollution and clogged drainage systems.

4. Limited recreational spaces – Some residents feel there are not enough parks or open areas 
   where families and youth can relax or exercise.

5. Youth engagement – Many young people need more programs, activities,
   and opportunities that help them develop their talents and leadership skills.

Did You Know?


Local Statistic:
Santo Niño is a barangay in the city of Marikina. Its population as determined by the 2020 Census was 28,849, which represents 6.33% of the total population of Marikina City.

Historical Fact:
Barangay Sto. Niño is one of the important communities in Marikina that contributes to the city's development and local programs for residents.

Fun Fact:
Sto. Niño Elementary School has been one of the key educational institutions in the community, helping shape the knowledge and values of many young residents.
