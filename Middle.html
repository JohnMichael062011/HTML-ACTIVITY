Long before the world learned to measure time, when the sky still listened closely to the earth, there lived a colossal bird named Minokawa beyond the eastern seas. Its wings were wide enough to darken the horizon, and its eyes glowed with the hunger of the cosmos itself. Minokawa watched the heavens in silence, fascinated by the radiant beauty of the sun and the gentle glow of the moon as they traveled across the sky. One day, a deep longing stirred within the creature. The light of the sun and moon called to Minokawa, not as enemies but as irresistible wonders. Drawn by their brilliance, the great bird rose from its distant realm and flew toward the sky, its shadow rippling across the oceans and lands below.
